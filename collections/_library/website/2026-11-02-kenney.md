Kenney, affectionately known as *Asset Jesus* in the indie game development community, creates free and affordable game assets that help developers bring their ideas to life.

His enormous collection includes 2D sprites, 3D models, interface elements and audio, covering everything from platform games to racing games. He also makes his own games and development tools, making his website an invaluable resource for anyone interested in game development.
