AudioMass is a free browser-based audio editor that feels a bit like Audacity, but runs entirely online. You can record, edit, trim, layer, and apply effects to audio without installing anything, making it surprisingly powerful for a web app.

It also supports multitrack editing, sample projects, and a growing collection of tools for music, podcasts, sound effects, and general audio work. Best of all, it’s [open source](https://github.com/pkalogiros/audiomass), so you can explore how it works or even contribute to its development yourself.
