Spelunkr is a free online TV service that streams archived television shows, cartoons and other videos from the Internet Archive. Rather than choosing what to watch, you tune into themed channels and see whatever happens to be playing.

With its retro web design and recordings that sometimes include original adverts, Spelunkr recreates the experience of watching television from years gone by. It's a creative way to rediscover old media and help preserve content that might otherwise be forgotten.
