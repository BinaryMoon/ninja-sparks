Tiny Little Office is a free, browser-based office suite featuring a word processor, spreadsheet, presentation tool and drawing app. Everything runs locally in your browser, with no account or cloud storage required. Its cleverest feature is the ability to share documents using links that contain the document itself, without needing a server to store your files. A simple, privacy-focused alternative to traditional online office suites.
