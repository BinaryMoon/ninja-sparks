## Free Education

I recently discovered that some US universities have made the course materials for their classes available online for free. Obviously this is not quite the same as a full education, you don't get the tutors, feedback, peer support, or the eventual qualification, but you do get access to the course materials and you can work through them at your own pace.

This is great for people who want to learn something new without the pressure of exams or the expense of a full education.

https://ocw.mit.edu/

MIT OpenCourseWare (OCW) is a web-based publication of virtually all MIT course content. OCW is open and available to the world and is a permanent MIT activity.
